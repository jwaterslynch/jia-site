How it works

Built to show where the paper stands, what it actually proves, and what would improve it.

01

Ingest and route

The manuscript is sanitized, digested, classified into an epistemic track, and routed into the right module set.

02

Independent reviews

Multiple models review the manuscript independently before seeing each other’s positions.

03

Evidence and checks

Claims are anchored to excerpts, references are checked, and track-specific auditors probe the paper’s weakest seams.

04

Debate and convergence

Reviewers respond to each other, record concessions, hold or change position, and expose the real disagreement structure.

05

Action-editor output

The final layer produces an editorial diagnosis, protected core, contested findings, and a revision path rather than a bare score.

Why this shape

JIA is designed as editorial diagnosis plus revision planning, with the verdict as a visible by-product.

Benchmarked against gold-standard human reviews on HR-2026-0228 and BJM-26-0178. Current result: 4/5 overall alignment, including 5/5 on theory-evidence calibration.

Evidence before posture

Claims are anchored to manuscript excerpts and retrieval-supported references instead of free-floating reviewer confidence.

Disagreement is surfaced

The system does not collapse conflict into a single hidden score. It shows who changed position, what they conceded, and what remains contested.

Revision paths are explicit

The author sees a protected core, a constrained diagnosis, and a concrete path toward a stronger next version.
